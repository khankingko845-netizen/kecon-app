## park-ambiences

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/park-ambiences

Uploader: Thimras

License declaration: License(s): CC0

Description excerpt: 3 recordings of ambience in different parts of a public park. One is close to a copse of trees, featuring birds chattering before the rain. The second is a recording of a small river with water falling over a low step, with some bird calls and frogs and other wildlife. The third is recorded in an open field with more intense wind. All recordings were made in a public park in Adelaide, South Australia, in winter. All files are recorded at 48kHz. To keep the filesize under 100mb, the two largest recordings have a bit depth of 16 bit. The park_ambience_wind file has a bit depth of 24 bit.

Source download links:
